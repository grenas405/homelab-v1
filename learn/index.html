The machine you just built

You cloned a repo and ran setup.sh. It put four things on your workstation that together let you chat with a language model that runs entirely on your own hardware. This handbook walks through each part: what it is, why it's there, and how to drive it.

Nothing here talks to the internet. Every page is one file. Read them in order or jump around.
