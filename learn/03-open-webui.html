The console — Open WebUI

Open WebUI is the chat website you open in a browser. It holds your conversations, settings, and account — but it never runs a model itself. Every message is handed to Ollama and the reply is streamed back.

Front end, not brain

Keep the split from Plate 02 in mind:

  • Open WebUI — the browser app at http://localhost:8080. Chat history, the model picker, system prompts, user accounts, settings.
  • Ollama — the server at http://localhost:11434 that actually loads and runs the model.

When you press enter, Open WebUI packages the conversation and sends it to Ollama, then displays the tokens as they come back. If Ollama is down or has no models, Open WebUI has nothing to show — which is why Plate 02's health check is the first thing to try when chat stops working.

The local account On first launch Open WebUI asks you to create an account with an email and password. That account lives in a database file on your workstation. It is not a login to any service, nothing is sent anywhere, and the "email" can be anything — it's just the username.

Starting and stopping it

setup.sh installs Open WebUI into a Python virtual environment at ~/openwebui-env. It does not leave it running. To start it — now, or any time after a reboot:

$ source ~/openwebui-env/bin/activate
(openwebui-env) $ open-webui serve

Then open http://localhost:8080. Leave that terminal open while you use it; press Ctrl-C in it to stop the server. The (openwebui-env) prefix tells you the virtual environment is active — Plate 05 explains what that environment is and why it's there.

The one-line version Open WebUI is the browser app on port 8080. It stores your chats and settings and forwards every message to Ollama on port 11434. Start it with activate then open-webui serve; stop it with Ctrl-C.

Self-check

1. Which task does Open WebUI do itself, without Ollama?

Open WebUI owns the accounts, history, and UI. Everything to do with running the model is Ollama's job.

2. You reboot the workstation and http://localhost:8080 won't load. Most likely cause?

Unlike the Ollama service, Open WebUI isn't started automatically. After a reboot you start it by hand.

3. Where does the account you create on first launch actually live?

It's a local account in a local database. The email field is just a username; nothing leaves the machine.
